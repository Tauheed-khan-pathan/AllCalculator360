About All Calculator 360

All Calculator 360 is a free collection of online calculators for finance, health, math and everyday life. Our goal is simple: every calculator you need, all in one place — fast, accurate and easy to use.

We are building towards 200+ calculators, starting with the ones people use most: EMI, SIP, FD, BMI, tax, age, percentages and more. Every calculator is free to use, with no sign-up and no downloads.

Privacy comes first. All calculations happen right in your browser — your numbers are never sent to a server, stored or shared. That is why the site works the same whether you use one calculator or a hundred.

All Calculator 360 is run by its owner as an independent project. If you have a suggestion or spot a mistake, we would love to hear from you — see our Contact page.
