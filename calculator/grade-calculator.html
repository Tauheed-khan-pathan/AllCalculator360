Grade Calculator Formula

Required final score = (desired grade − current grade × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight

Worked example: Current grade 80%, you want 85% overall, and the final is worth 40%: required score = (85 − 80 × 0.6) ÷ 0.4 = (85 − 48) ÷ 0.4 = 37 ÷ 0.4 = 92.5%. If the answer is over 100%, that target is not possible — you would need more than a perfect score. If it is below 0%, you have already reached your target even with a zero on the final.

Weighted grade = Σ(score × weight) ÷ Σ weights

Worked example: Homework 90 (20%), Quiz 80 (30%), Midterm 85 (50%): weighted grade = (90 × 20 + 80 × 30 + 85 × 50) ÷ 100 = (18 + 24 + 42.5) = 84.5%. Your weights should add up to 100%; if they do not, the calculator still divides by the total weight you entered, and warns you to check them.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my grade?

Add up each assignment's score multiplied by its weight, then divide by the total weight. For example, if homework is worth 20% and you scored 90, quizzes are worth 30% and you scored 80, and the midterm is worth 50% and you scored 85, your grade is (90 × 20 + 80 × 30 + 85 × 50) ÷ 100 = 84.5%. Use the weighted grade tool above — just enter each assignment's score and weight.

What score do I need on the final to pass?

It depends on your current grade, the grade you need, and how much the final is worth. Use the required final exam score tool above: enter your current grade, the grade you want, and the final's weight. For example, with a current grade of 80%, wanting 85% overall, and a final worth 40%, you need (85 − 80 × 0.6) ÷ 0.4 = 92.5% on the final.

What if my weights don't add to 100%?

The calculator still works: it divides by the total of the weights you entered, so the result is the weighted average of the assignments you listed. But if your weights should add to 100% and they do not, one weight is probably wrong or an assignment is missing, so check your syllabus and fix the weights before trusting the result.

What is a weighted grade?

A weighted grade gives some assignments more influence than others, based on how much each one counts towards your final grade. A midterm worth 50% counts five times as much as homework worth 10%, so a low midterm score hurts your grade far more than a low homework score. The weighted average is the sum of each score times its weight, divided by the total weight.

Can I still pass if my current grade is low?

Often yes — it depends on how much of the grade is still undecided and how high the pass mark is. If the final exam is worth a large share, a strong final can pull a low current grade up a lot. Enter your numbers in the required final exam score tool above: if it says you need more than 100%, passing at that target is not possible, so aim for the highest grade you can still reach and check your teacher's rules on retakes or extra credit.
