How to Calculate Time

To add or subtract durations by hand, work in seconds, minutes and hours separately and carry or borrow in steps of 60: 60 seconds make a minute and 60 minutes make an hour. Add the seconds first, carry any full 60 seconds into the minutes, then add the minutes and carry any full 60 minutes into the hours. Subtraction works the same way in reverse — borrow 60 seconds from a minute, or 60 minutes from an hour, when the top number is too small.

To find the gap between two clock times, subtract the start time from the end time. If the end time is earlier than the start, add 24 hours to it first — the gap runs overnight. Once you have a duration in hours and minutes, you can turn it into pay with the Hours Calculator, or measure a gap in days instead with the Date Calculator.

Time Formula

Total seconds = hours × 3,600 + minutes × 60 + seconds

Decimal hours = total seconds ÷ 3,600

Time between = end time − start time (add 24 hours if the end is earlier)

Time Example

Here are the two worked examples from the formulas above — first adding durations, then the gap between two clock times.

Worked example: 2 hours 45 minutes + 1 hour 30 minutes. In minutes that is 165 + 90 = 255 minutes, which is 4 hours 15 minutes. In seconds that is 255 × 60 = 15,300 seconds, and in decimal hours 255 ÷ 60 = 4.25 hours.

Worked example: From 9:00 to 17:30 is 17:30 − 9:00 = 8 hours 30 minutes, which is 510 minutes, 30,600 seconds, or 8.5 hours. And a useful conversion to remember: 90 minutes = 90 ÷ 60 = 1.5 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add two times together?

Add the seconds first, then the minutes, then the hours, carrying 60 seconds into a minute and 60 minutes into an hour. For example, 2 hours 45 minutes + 1 hour 30 minutes = 4 hours 15 minutes, because 45 + 30 = 75 minutes, which is 1 hour and 15 minutes. Use the add durations tool above — just enter both durations and choose Add.

How do I subtract one time from another?

Subtract the seconds, then the minutes, then the hours, borrowing 60 seconds from a minute or 60 minutes from an hour when you need to. For example, 5 hours 10 minutes − 2 hours 45 minutes = 2 hours 25 minutes, because borrowing 1 hour gives 70 − 45 = 25 minutes and 4 − 2 = 2 hours. If the result is negative, the second duration is longer than the first.

How many minutes are in 1.5 hours?

90 minutes. Multiply the hours by 60: 1.5 × 60 = 90 minutes. In general, decimal hours × 60 gives minutes, and minutes ÷ 60 gives decimal hours — so 45 minutes is 45 ÷ 60 = 0.75 hours.

How do I find the time between two clock times?

Subtract the start time from the end time. For example, from 9:00 to 17:30 is 8 hours 30 minutes, because 17:30 − 9:00 = 8 hours and 30 minutes. If the end time is earlier than the start time, this calculator treats it as the next day, so 22:00 to 6:00 is 8 hours.

What is decimal time?

Decimal time writes a duration as a single decimal number of hours instead of hours and minutes. For example, 4 hours 15 minutes is 4.25 hours, because 15 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.25 hours. Decimal hours are handy for multiplying by an hourly rate — the Hours Calculator uses them to work out pay.

Can I add times that go past 24 hours?

Yes. The add durations tool has no 24-hour limit, so adding 20 hours and 10 hours gives 30 hours. The time-between tool is different: it measures the gap between two clock times on the same day, or overnight to the next day, so its longest answer is just under 24 hours.
