How Dates Are Calculated

Days between = End date − Start date

Worked example: 8 November 2026 − 1 January 2026 = 311 days — that is 44 weeks and 3 days, or 10 months and 7 days.

New date = Start date ± Number of days

Worked example: 1 January 2026 + 100 days = 11 April 2026 (Saturday). January has 31 days, February 28 days in 2026, and March 31 days — 31 + 28 + 31 = 90 days takes you to 1 April, and 10 more days takes you to 11 April.

Leap years are counted automatically: 28 February 2024 + 1 day = 29 February 2024, because 2024 is a leap year.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I count the days between two dates?

Subtract the earlier date from the later date. For example, 1 January 2026 to 8 November 2026 is 311 days. This calculator counts the days in between and does not count the start date itself, so two dates one day apart are 1 day apart.

Does the calculator count leap years?

Yes. Leap years are counted automatically because the calculation uses real calendar dates, so 29 February is included whenever it falls between the two dates.

How do I add days to a date?

Enter a start date and the number of days, then choose Add. For example, 1 January 2026 plus 100 days is 11 April 2026, a Saturday.

What happens if the end date is before the start date?

The calculator shows the absolute number of days between the two dates, and notes that the end date is before the start date. Swap the dates if you want the order to run the other way.

Is my date stored anywhere?

No. All calculation happens in your browser, and your dates are never sent to a server or stored anywhere.
