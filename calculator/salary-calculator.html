How to Calculate Salary

A salary is one number wearing five outfits: annual, monthly, weekly, daily and hourly pay are all the same pay, just divided differently. The anchor is the annual salary. Monthly pay is the annual salary divided by 12, weekly pay is the annual salary divided by the weeks you work each year, daily pay divides it by the working days in the year, and hourly pay divides it by the total hours you work in the year. The only judgement call is how many weeks and hours you count — this calculator uses your own hours per week and weeks per year, so a teacher on a 40-week year and an office worker on a 52-week year get different hourly rates from the same annual salary, as they should.

Take-home pay is the same idea with one extra step: subtract tax and deductions first. Enter your total deduction as a single percentage and every figure — annual, monthly, weekly, daily and hourly — is multiplied by what is left. If your hours drive your pay rather than your salary, the Hours Calculator works out daily and weekly pay from your start and finish times, and if you want the exact Indian income tax on your salary, use the Income Tax Calculator and enter that tax percentage here.

Salary Formula

Monthly pay = annual salary ÷ 12

Weekly pay = annual salary ÷ weeks per year

Daily pay = annual salary ÷ (weeks per year × 5)

Hourly pay = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year)

Take-home pay = gross pay × (1 − deduction % ÷ 100)

If your pay is given monthly, weekly, daily or hourly instead of annually, the calculator first converts it to an annual salary — monthly × 12, weekly × weeks per year, daily × weeks per year × 5, hourly × hours per week × weeks per year — and then works out all the other figures from that annual number.

Salary Example

Worked example: A salary of $60,000 a year, working 40 hours a week for 52 weeks: monthly pay = 60,000 ÷ 12 = $5,000.00; weekly pay = 60,000 ÷ 52 = $1,153.85; daily pay = 60,000 ÷ 260 = $230.77; hourly pay = 60,000 ÷ (40 × 52) = 60,000 ÷ 2,080 = $28.85.

Worked example: The same $60,000 salary with 20% tax and deductions: take-home pay = 60,000 × 0.80 = $48,000.00 a year, $4,000.00 a month and $923.08 a week.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my hourly rate from my annual salary?

Divide your annual salary by the number of hours you work in a year: hours per week × weeks per year. For $60,000 a year at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, that is 60,000 ÷ (40 × 52) = 60,000 ÷ 2,080 = $28.85 an hour. Enter your salary and hours above and the calculator shows it instantly.

How much is $60,000 a year per month?

$5,000 a month before tax: 60,000 ÷ 12 = 5,000. Per week it is $1,153.85 (60,000 ÷ 52), per day $230.77 (60,000 ÷ 260 working days) and per hour $28.85 at 40 hours a week. With a 20% deduction, take-home pay is $4,000 a month.

How is take-home pay calculated?

Take-home pay is your gross pay minus tax and other deductions. Enter your total deduction as a percentage — for example 20% — and the calculator multiplies every figure by 0.80. At $60,000 with 20% deducted, take-home pay is $48,000 a year, or $4,000 a month.

What does the daily pay assume?

Daily pay assumes a 5-day working week, so it divides your annual salary by weeks per year × 5. With 52 weeks that is 260 working days: $60,000 ÷ 260 = $230.77 a day. If you work a different number of days, change the weeks-per-year figure to match your schedule.

Can I use this calculator for a monthly or hourly salary?

Yes — choose your pay frequency at the top. Enter a monthly salary and it converts it to annual (× 12), weekly and hourly pay; enter an hourly rate and it works out the annual salary from your hours per week and weeks per year.

Does this calculator include tax?

No — tax rules differ by country and income, so the calculator does not apply any tax table. Enter your own tax and deduction percentage to see take-home pay, or use the Income Tax Calculator to work out Indian income tax for FY 2025-26 and enter that percentage here.
