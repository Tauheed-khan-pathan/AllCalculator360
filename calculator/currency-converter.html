How to Calculate Currency Conversion

Converting money is one multiplication: take the amount, multiply it by the exchange rate between the two currencies, and you have the converted amount. The only hard part is the rate itself, because rates move every day. This page keeps the rate in your hands — the table on the left holds the rate of one US dollar in each currency, and you can change any of them. If the rupee rate moves from 88.70 to 89.20 tomorrow, type 89.20 in and every result on the page updates at once. That is the same idea as the Unit Converter, which converts one value into every unit at once — here the amount is shown in all nine currencies in the table on the right.

When you convert for a real payment, remember that the converted figure is the rate before bank charges. Banks and transfer services add their own margin and often a fee, so the money that actually arrives is a little less. Check the rate your bank offers, type it into the rate table, and the result will match what your bank would give before its fee. Currency Conversion Formula

Converted amount = amount × (rate of To currency ÷ rate of From currency)

Reverse rate = 1 ÷ exchange rate

Each rate in the table is how many units of that currency one US dollar buys. So the rate from any currency to any other is the To rate divided by the From rate. For example, the USD to INR rate is 88.70 ÷ 1 = 88.70, and the EUR to USD rate is 1 ÷ 0.86 = 1.1628, so €100 becomes $116.28.

Currency Conversion Example

Worked example: Convert $100 to Indian rupees at 88.70 rupees per dollar: $100 × 88.70 = ₹8,870. The reverse rate is 1 ÷ 88.70 = 0.0113, so 1 INR = $0.0113, and ₹8,870 back to dollars is 8,870 ÷ 88.70 = $100.

Worked example: Convert €100 to US dollars at 0.86 euros per dollar: €100 ÷ 0.86 = $116.28. And £50 to euros: £50 × (0.86 ÷ 0.75) = 50 × 1.1467 = €57.33.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert USD to INR?

Multiply the dollar amount by the USD to INR rate. With the rate of 88.70 rupees per dollar, $100 × 88.70 = ₹8,870. Enter the amount above, choose USD and INR, and the calculator does it instantly — and if today's rate is different, edit the rate in the table to match it.

Are the exchange rates on this page live?

No — this page cannot fetch live rates, because it runs entirely in your browser with no server. The rates shown are dated 10 Nov 2026, and every rate is editable: check today's rate from your bank or a currency website, type it into the rate table, and all conversions update instantly.

How do I convert INR back to USD?

Divide the rupee amount by the USD to INR rate, or use the reverse rate shown in the results. At 88.70, ₹8,870 ÷ 88.70 = $100, and 1 INR = $0.0113. The swap button above exchanges the two currencies in one click.

Why does my bank give a different amount?

Banks and money-transfer services add their own margin to the mid-market rate and may charge a fee, so the amount you actually receive is usually a little less than the converted figure here. This calculator uses the mid-market style rate you enter, so treat the result as the rate before bank charges.

Which currencies can I convert?

Nine currencies: US Dollar (USD), Indian Rupee (INR), Euro (EUR), British Pound (GBP), UAE Dirham (AED), Saudi Riyal (SAR), Japanese Yen (JPY), Australian Dollar (AUD) and Canadian Dollar (CAD). Pick any two — the calculator converts in either direction.

How do I convert a large amount, like $10,000?

Type the full amount — there is no limit. $10,000 at 88.70 is ₹8,87,000. For very large transfers, check your bank's rate first and edit the rate in the table, because even a small rate difference changes the result by thousands of rupees.
