How to Calculate Age

Your age is simply the time between your date of birth and another date — usually today. The tricky part is that months and years are not all the same length, so you cannot just divide your total days by 365 and call it done. A proper age calculation counts whole calendar years first, then whole months, and only then the leftover days, borrowing from the previous month or year when the day or month has not been reached yet.

That is also why two people born a few days apart can be the same age in years but differ in months and days, and why your age in total days is a useful extra number — it is exact, while years, months and days are a calendar breakdown of the same span. If you need the gap between any two dates rather than an age, the Date Calculator counts days between dates and adds or subtracts days from a date.

Your age is found by comparing your date of birth with the chosen date, counting full years first, then full months, then the remaining days. If the day of the month has not been reached yet, days are borrowed from the previous month — for example, if you were born on the 29th and today is the 8th, the days are borrowed from the previous month and one month is taken away. If the birth month has not been reached yet, 12 months are borrowed and one year is taken away.

Leap years are counted automatically, because the calculation uses real calendar dates — so 29 February and every extra leap day are included in your total days lived.

Age Calculator Formula

Age = “Age at” date − Date of birth, counted in whole years, then whole months, then remaining days

If the day of the month has not been reached yet, borrow days from the previous month and take away one month; if the birth month has not been reached yet, borrow 12 months and take away one year. Leap years need no special step, because the count runs on real calendar dates.

Age Calculator Example

Worked example: Date of birth 1 January 2000, as of 8 November 2026 → 26 years, 10 months, 7 days; 9,808 days lived; born on a Saturday.

How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth in the “Date of Birth” field.

  4. Change either date to compare ages — for example, how old you will be on a future birthday, or how old someone was on a given date. You can also pair it with the BMI Calculator for a quick health check at the same age.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is age calculated?

Your age is calculated by comparing your date of birth with the 'Age at' date, counting full years first, then full months, then the remaining days. If the day of the month has not been reached yet, days are borrowed from the previous month, and if the birth month has not been reached yet, months are borrowed from the previous year.

Does the calculator count leap years?

Yes. Leap years are counted automatically because the calculation uses real calendar dates, so 29 February and the extra day in leap years are included in your total days lived.

What if I was born on 29 February?

If you were born on 29 February, your birthday falls on 29 February in leap years. In non-leap years it is commonly celebrated on 28 February or 1 March; this calculator uses 1 March for your next birthday in non-leap years.

Can I calculate my age at a past or future date?

Yes. Change the 'Age at' date to any past or future date to see how old you were, or will be, on that date — as long as it is on or after your date of birth.

Is my date of birth stored anywhere?

No. All calculation happens in your browser, and your date of birth is never sent to a server or stored anywhere.

How old am I in days?

Enter your date of birth and the calculator shows your total days lived next to your age in years, months and days. For example, someone born on 1 January 2000 had lived 9,808 days by 8 November 2026. Your total days is the exact count, including every leap day.
