Conversion Formulas

Length: value in metres = value × factor  •  Weight: value in kg = value × factor  •  Volume: value in litres = value × factor

Worked example — length: 5 miles = 5 × 1,609.344 m = 8,046.72 m = 8.04672 km.

Worked example — temperature: 100 °C = 100 × 9 ÷ 5 + 32 = 212 °F. 32 °F = (32 − 32) × 5 ÷ 9 = 0 °C, and 0 °C = 0 + 273.15 = 273.15 K.

Worked example — weight: 1 kg = 1 ÷ 0.45359237 = 2.20462 lb. Worked example — volume: 1 US gallon = 3.78541 L, and 2 L = 2,000 mL.

Exact factors used: 1 mile = 1,609.344 m; 1 yard = 0.9144 m; 1 foot = 0.3048 m; 1 inch = 0.0254 m; 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg; 1 ounce = 0.028349523125 kg; 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 L; 1 US cup = 0.2365882365 L.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the unit converter work?

Pick a category, enter a value, and choose the units to convert from and to. The value is converted through a standard base unit — metres for length, kilograms for weight, and litres for volume — except temperature, which uses the Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin formulas directly.

How do I convert miles to kilometres?

Multiply miles by 1.609344. For example, 5 miles × 1.609344 = 8.04672 km. One mile is exactly 1,609.344 metres.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32. For example, 100 °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32 = 212 °F. To go back, subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9.

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

One kilogram is 2.20462 pounds. One pound is exactly 0.45359237 kilograms.

Is my converted value stored anywhere?

No. All conversion happens in your browser, and your values are never sent to a server or stored anywhere.
