Sales Tax Calculator Formula

Tax amount = price before tax × tax rate ÷ 100

Total price = price before tax + tax amount

Worked example: A $100 item at 8% sales tax: tax = $100 × 8 ÷ 100 = $8.00; total = $100 + $8.00 = $108.00.

Price before tax = price including tax ÷ (1 + tax rate ÷ 100)

Tax amount = price including tax − price before tax

Worked example: A $108 total at 8% sales tax: price before tax = $108 ÷ (1 + 8 ÷ 100) = $108 ÷ 1.08 = $100.00; tax = $108.00 − $100.00 = $8.00.

Third example: A $59.99 item at 6.25% sales tax: tax = $59.99 × 6.25 ÷ 100 = $3.75; total = $59.99 + $3.75 = $63.74.

This calculator has no built-in state table: US sales tax varies by state, from 0% to over 10% once local taxes are included, so enter your own local rate. Five states (Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon) have no statewide sales tax.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is sales tax?

Sales tax is a tax charged by state and local governments on the sale of goods and some services, added on top of the price at the checkout. The seller collects it from the buyer and passes it on to the government. Unlike VAT or GST, sales tax is charged only once, at the final sale to the consumer, and the rate depends on where the purchase happens.

How is sales tax calculated?

Multiply the pre-tax price by the tax rate divided by 100. For example, a $100 item at 8% sales tax has tax of $100 × 8 ÷ 100 = $8.00, so the total you pay is $100 + $8.00 = $108.00. To work backwards from a price that already includes tax, divide the total by (1 + tax rate ÷ 100) — for example, $108 ÷ 1.08 = $100 before tax, so the tax was $8.00.

Which US states have no sales tax?

Five US states have no statewide sales tax: Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon. Alaska has no state sales tax, but some local areas in Alaska do charge a local sales tax. Every other state charges sales tax, and the combined state-plus-local rate varies from 0% in some places to over 10% in others, so always enter your local rate.

What is the difference between sales tax and VAT or GST?

Sales tax is charged only once, at the final retail sale to the consumer. VAT (value added tax) and GST (goods and services tax) are charged at every stage of production and distribution, with businesses claiming back the tax they paid on their purchases, so only the final consumer bears the full tax. The US uses sales tax; most other countries, including the UK, EU countries and India, use VAT or GST instead.

How do I back out tax from a total?

Divide the total price by (1 + tax rate ÷ 100) to get the pre-tax price, then subtract that from the total to get the tax amount. For example, a $108 total at 8% tax gives a pre-tax price of $108 ÷ 1.08 = $100.00, so the tax included was $108 − $100 = $8.00. The second tool on this page does exactly this calculation for you.
