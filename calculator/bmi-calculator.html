How to Calculate BMI

BMI — Body Mass Index — is a quick screening number that relates your weight to your height. It does not measure body fat directly; it simply asks whether your weight is in the usual range for someone of your height. That makes it useful as a first check, and as a number to track over time, but it is not a diagnosis on its own.

The categories are the same for most adults: below 18.5 is underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 is normal, 25 to 29.9 is overweight, and 30 or above is obese. Because BMI ignores muscle, it can overstate weight for very muscular people and understate it for people who have lost muscle, so athletes and older adults should read it alongside other measures. Your healthy weight range comes from the same formula run backwards — the weights that would put your BMI at 18.5 and at 24.9. To see how many calories your body burns at rest, check your result against the BMR Calculator, and for a daily calorie target use the Calorie Calculator.

BMI Formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)2

BMI Calculator Example

Divide your weight in kilograms by your height in metres squared. For example, a person who weighs 70 kg and is 1.75 m tall: BMI = 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 70 ÷ 3.0625 = 22.86 — Normal.

Healthy weight range for 1.75 m: 18.5 × (1.75 × 1.75) = 56.7 kg up to 24.9 × (1.75 × 1.75) = 76.3 kg, so 56.7–76.3 kg.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a simple number calculated from your height and weight that shows whether your weight is in a healthy range for your height.

What is a healthy BMI?

A healthy BMI for most adults is between 18.5 and 24.9. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25 to 29.9 is overweight, and 30 or above is obese.

Is BMI accurate for athletes or very muscular people?

Not always. Muscle weighs more than fat, so a very muscular person can have a high BMI even with little body fat. BMI is a screening tool, not a direct measure of body fat.

Is BMI different for children?

Yes. Children and teenagers use age-and-sex percentile charts to interpret BMI, not the adult cut-offs of 18.5, 25 and 30.

How can I reach a healthy weight range?

A balanced diet and regular physical activity are the usual starting points. Small, steady changes work better than crash diets. This calculator is for information only and is not medical advice.

What BMI is healthy for my height?

A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is the healthy range for most adults. Your healthy weight in kilograms is 18.5 × your height in metres squared up to 24.9 × your height in metres squared — for example, 56.7 to 76.3 kg at 1.75 m. This calculator shows your range automatically.
